# Website Ventures — Acquisition Playbook v1

**Updated: 2026-09-08 · ChatGPT (OpenAI)**

Purpose: get Website Ventures to its first 10 paying customers without building the business around legally risky mass cold outreach or an evening-heavy call centre routine.

## 1. Legal boundary — Netherlands, current as of 2026-09-08

This is an operational compliance summary, not legal advice.

### Telephone

Since **1 July 2026**, consumers and small/natural-person entrepreneurs such as **zzp/sole proprietors and vof's** may only be called for a commercial offer when they gave **prior explicit consent**. The ACM says the caller must be able to prove that consent.

Operational rule for Website Ventures:
- do **not** cold-call a sole proprietor, vof, maatschap or consumer without documented consent;
- do not assume an old customer relationship is enough after 1 July 2026;
- company-form checking is not a substitute for professional legal review if outbound calling later becomes a major channel.

### Email / WhatsApp / electronic messages

ACM's spam rules say commercial electronic messages should only be sent to recipients who gave prior permission, apart from limited existing-customer exceptions. The sender must be clear and unsubscribing must be easy.

Operational rule:
- no scraped mass cold email;
- no unsolicited WhatsApp sales blasts;
- do not treat a publicly listed email address as consent;
- a platform user who posted a website job is different: reply **inside the platform/request flow** according to that platform's rules rather than exporting their details to a private cold list.

### Personal data

KVK notes that direct marketing also has AVG/privacy boundaries. Data that can be traced to a person may be personal data even when found through business sources.

Operational rule:
- store only what is needed to qualify/follow up a legitimate opportunity;
- do not build large personal-data prospect databases just because information is publicly findable.

## 2. Channel order for Joey's first 10 clients

### P0 — Active-buyer marketplaces

Use buyers who already raised their hand and asked for website help.

Current evidence:
- Freelancer.nl currently exposes active `Website` / `Website maken` jobs.
- Hoofdkraan.nl exposes `Websites & Applicaties` / `Web Design` jobs and supports responding to listed assignments.

Why this suits Joey:
- can be handled after work;
- the buyer already has intent;
- no mass outbound list needed;
- response quality matters more than call volume;
- the Plumbing demo / Agency Showroom can become proof rather than a generic CV.

Initial rule:
- review opportunities **2 evenings per week**;
- reply only to strong-fit jobs, not everything;
- maximum **3 thoughtful applications per session**;
- log every response in `website-ventures-acquisition-tracker.html`.

### P0 — Warm introductions

Sources:
- colleagues/friends/family who know a local business owner;
- existing professional network;
- tradespeople encountered naturally;
- customer referral after delivery.

Ask for an introduction, not a contact dump.

Simple approach:
> “Ken jij toevallig een bedrijf dat binnenkort een nieuwe site nodig heeft? Als je ze kent, wil je ons gewoon aan elkaar voorstellen?”

The person being introduced has context for why Joey is contacting them.

### P1 — Referral flywheel

Every successful handoff should trigger one operational step:
- ask whether the customer knows **one or two businesses** that would benefit from the same treatment;
- make forwarding the Agency/Plumbing showcase easy;
- log referral source so later conversion can be measured.

Do not promise referral payments until the economics are understood.

### P1 — In-person local discovery

A small, deliberate local visit can be useful where it is socially appropriate and the business is open — especially when Joey can show a polished demo immediately.

This is **not** permission to conduct aggressive door-to-door consumer selling. Treat it as relationship-building with businesses and stop immediately when there is no interest.

### P2 — Outbound telephone to incorporated businesses

Do not make this a core channel yet.

The specific ACM opt-in telemarketing protection is written around consumers and other natural persons / small entrepreneurs. A BV or other legal entity can sit differently under that rule, but this is not a good reason to build a fragile loophole-based growth system. If this later becomes material, re-check current law and get professional advice on exact scope, company-form verification, data processing and call practice.

### EXCLUDE by default

- scraped cold-email campaigns;
- cold WhatsApp campaigns;
- calling zzp/vof leads without explicit documented consent;
- bought lead databases without verified legal basis;
- automation that sends sales messages just because a business has public contact data.

## 3. First-10-client operating loop

### Step A — qualify opportunity

Strong-fit first customers:
- business actually needs a website/new website;
- scope can be delivered with the reusable master system;
- customer can provide truthful business/service/contact information;
- no complex webapp/e-commerce/custom integration hidden inside a cheap site request;
- decision maker is reachable through a legitimate channel;
- budget is not obviously incompatible with the current founding-offer hypothesis.

### Step B — response

Do not lead with “I can build a website.” Lead with the outcome and proof.

Response structure:
1. one sentence showing you understood their business/request;
2. one specific improvement you would make;
3. one link/screenshot proving visual capability;
4. a bounded offer to make the next step easy;
5. one short question required to scope it.

Do not claim past client results that do not exist.

### Step C — before accepting

Run:
1. `website-ventures-client-intake.html`
2. `website-ventures-offer-builder.html`
3. explicit acceptance of price/scope through the chosen real commercial process
4. only then Client Factory / production.

### Step D — delivery

Use the existing flow:

**Intake → Offer Draft → Client Factory → real assets/proof → Selected Assets → Fulfilment Run → QA → Delivery Gate → commercial hosting/domain → launch → Customer Handoff.**

### Step E — after delivery

- request honest feedback/testimonial;
- request one warm referral;
- record active production minutes;
- record acquisition source and conversion;
- only then decide whether to repeat/raise price/change channel.

## 4. Weekly cadence while Joey works full-time

Default experiment, not a permanent commitment:

- **Tuesday evening — 35 min:** scan active-buyer platforms; save max 5 relevant jobs; send max 3 strong responses.
- **Thursday evening — 20 min:** follow legitimate open opportunities and warm introductions; no unsolicited electronic chasing where consent/legal basis is absent.
- **Weekend — 45 min:** improve one proof/demo element based on objections seen that week, not random redesign work.

Target is not 100 messages. Target is learning which 5–10 serious opportunities convert.

## 5. Metrics for customer 1–10

Track:
- channel;
- opportunity type;
- date discovered;
- legal/contact basis;
- quoted price;
- reply received yes/no;
- conversation yes/no;
- sale yes/no;
- revenue;
- active fulfilment minutes;
- revisions/change requests;
- referral generated yes/no.

After 10 paying customers calculate:
- conversion by channel;
- revenue per active production hour;
- acquisition effort per win;
- average revision burden;
- referral rate;
- whether €349 was too low/high for actual effort.

## 6. Current platform evidence

As checked on 2026-09-08:
- Freelancer.nl showed **15 active Website assignments** and 14 under `Website maken` in the search result pages.
- Hoofdkraan.nl exposes an active `Websites & Applicaties` marketplace and a freelancer response flow; some listings show budgets from hundreds of euros upward.

These counts change continuously. Treat them as evidence that active-buyer inventory exists, not as guaranteed future volume.

## 7. Decision rule

Do not invest in large-scale outbound tooling until:
1. the Agency Showroom is credible;
2. Plumbing proof/template is credible;
3. the offer/scope is locked;
4. at least a handful of real buyer conversations reveal what actually blocks sales.

Website Ventures should scale a conversion path that has evidence, not an imagined sales machine.
