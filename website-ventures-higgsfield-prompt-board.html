Round 01 · vier werelden

Startinstelling: Soul 2.0 · 16:9 · 2K · bij voorkeur Unlimited op higgsfield.ai. Maak per richting 3–4 varianten, maar beoordeel eerst de richting, niet kleine details.

A · White Precision

Porsche service × Apple

Helder wit · koel steen · chrome · glas · diep rood · technische precisie.
Ultra-premium visual identity study for a next-generation digital design agency. Pristine white architectural gallery, cool white stone, precision-machined chrome, crystal-clear glass and one deep oxblood-red accent material. Calm negative space, exact geometry, expensive automotive campaign discipline, Apple-level restraint, ultra-clean European luxury, photorealistic materials, soft directional studio light, subtle water reflections, sophisticated not flashy, no text, no logos, no people, no generic office, no stock-photo aesthetic, widescreen 16:9 composition with generous headline-safe negative space.
Reject: sterile dentist/clinic feeling, cheap red gradients, sci-fi neon, obvious 3D render look.
Winner if: “duur + exact + rustig” is immediately readable before any copy.
B · Warm Architectural

Architect studio × boutique hotel

Ivoor · travertin · warm licht · donker bordeaux · zachte materialiteit.
Ultra-premium visual identity study for a boutique digital design agency. Warm ivory architectural space, honed travertine, creamy limestone, smoked glass, brushed metal and restrained dark burgundy accents. Soft late-afternoon architectural light, quiet shadows, tactile natural materials, refined European hotel and architecture-studio luxury, editorial photography rather than showroom CGI, serene but expensive, generous negative space for web typography, no text, no logos, no people, no beige-on-beige blandness, no boho styling, no generic luxury apartment, widescreen 16:9.
Reject: Pinterest beige, wellness-spa cliché, farmhouse, overly soft/feminine lifestyle imagery.
Winner if: warm and aspirational but still feels like a serious high-performance agency.
C · Hybrid Precision Luxury

White precision + warm material richness

Huidige favoriet als synthese: wit/rood signatuur met steen, water, glas en chrome.
High-end campaign world for an elite web design agency, combining clinical precision with warm architectural luxury. Large white negative space, pale natural stone and travertine, polished chrome, clear glass, liquid water, and one signature deep oxblood-red translucent element. Extremely controlled composition, premium automotive-product photography meets contemporary architecture, warm highlights against cool clean whites, realistic tactile materials, understated drama, expensive and modern without looking like a fashion brand, no text, no logos, no people, no clutter, no neon, no generic tech gradients, widescreen 16:9 with clean copy-safe space.
Reject: too beige, too clinical, too much red, generic SaaS/AI aesthetic.
Winner if: combines the authority of A with the material richness of B without feeling indecisive.
D · Dark Tech

VOZOL effort × automotive launch

Charcoal · glossy glas · metaal · controlled red light · meeste drama.
Dramatic premium campaign identity for a cutting-edge digital design agency. Deep charcoal architectural space, black glass, satin metal, glossy reflections and controlled oxblood-red illumination. Precision automotive launch photography, cinematic depth, expensive high-tech product reveal, sharp pools of light, immaculate surfaces, restrained futuristic atmosphere, high contrast but still sophisticated, no text, no logos, no people, no gaming aesthetic, no cyberpunk neon, no RGB, no cheap black-and-red esports look, widescreen 16:9 with intentional negative space for headline copy.
Reject: gaming/esports, nightclub, cyberpunk, aggressive red everywhere.
Winner if: dramatic “holy shit” effect without sacrificing trust or timelessness.

Round 02 · pas na richtingkeuze

Gebruik deze drie concepten binnen de gekozen art direction. Ze testen betekenis, niet alleen schoonheid.

Hero 1

Transformation Sculpture

Hero campaign image for an elite digital design agency. In a pristine architectural gallery, one monumental sculptural form visibly transitions from raw unfinished neutral stone into immaculate polished chrome, crystal-clear glass and a controlled deep oxblood-red translucent material. The transformation is elegant and physically believable, symbolizing an ordinary business becoming a category-leading premium brand. Ultra-real material detail, restrained automotive campaign lighting, modern European luxury, large clean negative space for website headline and CTA, no text, no logo, no people, widescreen 16:9.
Meaning: ordinary → premium. Best if the image itself explains our product.
Hero 2

Website as Luxury Product

Hero image for a premium web design agency, treating a website interface as a luxury engineered product rather than a flat screenshot. A razor-thin floating glass display object with subtle chrome edges and a restrained deep-red accent, staged on a sculptural natural-stone plinth inside a pristine architectural space. Sophisticated product-launch photography, tactile realistic materials, minimal composition, huge negative space for headline copy, elegant reflections, no readable UI text, no brand logos, no people, no generic laptop mockup, widescreen 16:9.
Meaning: a website is a premium product, not a cheap document.
Hero 3

Material Performance

Abstract but photoreal premium hero for a high-end web design agency: macro architectural composition of flowing clear water crossing polished chrome, pale stone and deep oxblood-red glass, with precise controlled reflections and cinematic studio lighting. The materials feel engineered, tactile and extremely expensive; dynamic enough to imply motion but calm enough for a luxury homepage. Large copy-safe negative space, no text, no logos, no people, no decorative clutter, widescreen 16:9.
Meaning is weaker, but visual impact can be strongest. Use only if it genuinely elevates the coded hero.

Plumbing · technician exploration

Niet meteen een “character universe” bouwen. Eerst 4 geloofwaardige kandidaten, dan één winner als recurring technician.

Candidate round

Porsche treatment, authentic tradesperson

Photoreal campaign portrait and working-scene study of a real experienced European plumbing/service technician, approximately 30–45 years old, calm, competent and approachable. He wears premium charcoal or deep navy technical workwear with a very subtle oxblood-red accent line; the uniform is immaculate but believable, functional rather than fashionable. Authentic face, realistic hands, professional tools, natural posture while diagnosing a plumbing issue in a bright modern bathroom. Lighting and composition have the polish of a Porsche service campaign, but he must still feel like a real Dutch tradesperson rather than a fashion model. No hard hat unless context requires it, no suit, no crossed-arms corporate pose, no exaggerated stock-photo smile, no hyper-muscular model, no dirty stereotype, no text, no logos.
Choose one person only if he feels trustworthy enough for a real Dutch customer and premium enough to carry the whole flagship.

ChatGPT Work + Unlimited verification

Work kan op ondersteunde ingelogde websites klikken en typen. Maar we behandelen Unlimited via browser automation als onbewezen totdat we één gecontroleerde test hebben gedaan.

One-generation proof test after subscription
  1. Open ChatGPT Work and instruct it to use the Higgsfield website UI, not the Higgsfield plugin/MCP.
  2. Sign in to Higgsfield through Work's secure browser flow.
  3. Open Soul 2.0 and verify the Unlimited toggle is visibly active before generating.
  4. Record the starting credit balance.
  5. Generate exactly ONE A/B/C/D direction still.
  6. Open Higgsfield Manage Account → Usage and verify whether credits were deducted.
  7. If 0 credits were deducted: Work-browser workflow is approved for Unlimited exploration. If credits were deducted or the toggle cannot be used: stop and use manual website generation for Unlimited.
Do not infer. Higgsfield explicitly says plugin/MCP/CLI-style generations use credits. A browser-operated website generation may behave differently, so we prove it once with the usage log before scaling.

Internal production IP. Prompts are starting hypotheses, not public copy or client proof. No generation should be presented as completed real client work.
