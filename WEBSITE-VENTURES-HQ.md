## PURPOSE

Build and prove a repeatable website-sales business. Gamenfy is Joey's Life OS; Website Ventures is a commercial venture that needs a clear offer, repeatable sales process, scalable production, and evidence before heavy design/animation investment.

## NOW

1. Define the first offer in one sentence.
2. Pick the first narrow prospect segment rather than targeting all SMEs at once.
3. Define the first ten-customer production/sales loop.
4. Decide what the customer is really buying: speed, design, no-hassle setup, conversion/leads, maintenance, or a combination.
5. Validate pricing and recurring-revenue options before building a complex scale model.

## OPEN PRODUCT / BUSINESS QUESTIONS

- Exact first target niche?
- Exact launch offer and scope?
- One-off vs recurring monthly component?
- Which parts can be standardized without making the result look templated?
- What evidence/demo is required before cold calling?
- What should be automated with AI/Higgsfield and what should remain human-controlled?
- At what proof point should students/builders be recruited?
- At what proof point should sales be delegated?

## CURRENT DIRECTION

- Initial strategy: sell relatively affordable, better-looking websites to SMEs.
- Joey initially considered low entry prices and scaling through many customers.
- Long-term scale idea includes students/builders for production and eventually a sales layer.
- Strong commercial hero and Apple-like scroll/storytelling are desired, but should serve the offer rather than precede offer validation.
- Higgsfield/AI animation is a tool, not the business model.

## IDEA TANK

- Province-by-province rollout only after unit economics and sales conversion are proven.
- Use a repeatable visual/content system while retaining enough customization to feel bespoke.
- Consider recurring hosting/maintenance/Google-profile/updates as potentially more valuable than ultra-low one-off sale price.
- Keep the Website Ventures workspace linked from Gamenfy later so Joey sees next milestone and business progress without mixing the two projects.

## DECISIONS

- Website Ventures remains conceptually separate from Gamenfy even if both can appear in one Ventures cockpit.
- Do not optimize for 100,000 customers before proving customers 1-10.
- Do not let beautiful animation/design distract from offer, niche, economics, and sales proof.
