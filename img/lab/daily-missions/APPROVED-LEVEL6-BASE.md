## Style lock

Use the **dark carnival / night Park-D presentation** from the approved image as the visual family reference:

- premium rounded 3D animated-film / toy-like rendering;
- dark navy / purple night environment as the presentation reference, not a pastel daylight redesign;
- big expressive eyes, readable faces and compact silhouettes;
- cute and playful, but with enough detail that Level 6 already feels developed;
- characters should look like they belong to one game universe even when their bodies are very different;
- strong mission readability from silhouette/body concept;
- accessories support the mission but should not be the only thing communicating it;
- evolution should exaggerate the existing identity instead of replacing the character with a new one;
- Level 10 must become deliberately over-the-top / Master-tier.

## Approved Level 6 lineup

This is the visual lineup to preserve while isolating characters and creating the 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 evolution stages.

### 1. Nutrition

**Approved identity:** living stomach companion.  
Pink stomach-shaped body, large food/nutrition opening/bowl area with healthy food visible, green detail/cape accents, strong little limbs. It should remain clearly a stomach first, not become a generic vegetable mascot.

### 2. Brush Teeth

**Approved identity:** living tooth companion.  
White glossy molar body, blue accents/cape and toothbrush. Keep the tooth itself as the body and preserve the friendly heroic energy.

### 3. Household

**Approved identity:** human maid / household helper.  
This is intentionally one of the few human companions. Dark hair, maid/cleaning uniform, spray bottle and cloth. Keep it tasteful, cute and game-like; the archetype is a household helper, not a sexualized design.

### 4. Gratitude

**Approved identity:** glowing golden spirit / gratitude being.  
Warm golden body with gentle glow, calm grateful expression, small floating heart/energy accents. It should feel spiritual/positive rather than simply being another red heart.

### 5. Good Deed

**Approved identity:** real red heart superhero companion.  
Red anatomical-symbolic heart silhouette simplified into a cute character, confident heroic posture, cape/hero details. This character owns the explicit heart identity in the family.

### 6. Screen Time

**Approved identity:** compact retro screen/robot companion.  
Dark face screen with large green/blue eyes, metallic grey rounded device body, small antenna, robotic arms/legs. Lower display communicates balanced use with a restrained coloured meter and `2h 15m` in the approved concept. Avoid turning this into a normal smartphone with human limbs; it should feel like a distinct little device creature.

### 7. Cold Shower

**Approved identity:** crystalline ice/cold-water elemental.  
Blue translucent body, strong ice spikes/facets, water droplets, purple/blue game accents and an energetic expression. Keep the body itself elemental. Evolution should move from timid cold/water creature toward an outrageous crystalline Master guardian.

### 8. No Weed

**Approved identity:** green plant/forest guardian.  
Leafy green body/head silhouette, darker forest-green outfit/cape, stop/discipline gesture and confident expression. It should remain an original plant being rather than a literal cannabis-leaf icon mascot.

### 9. Private discipline / No Porn mission

**Approved identity:** half-demon / half-angel human transition character.  
This is intentionally one of the few human companions. At low level the evolution begins devilish; by Level 6 it is visibly transitioning toward angelic: one dark/demon side, one light/angel side, horn + halo / wing contrast. **Do not put the words `No Porn` on the character, shield or visible public-facing art.** Use neutral internal/presentation wording such as `Discipline` when a label is necessary.

### 10. Sleep

**Approved identity:** blond male sleeper.  
This is intentionally human. Blond male character, purple star/moon sleeping clothes, soft cloud/pillow volume, peaceful sleeping pose. Do not revert to the earlier female version unless Joey asks.

### 11. Steps

**Approved identity:** teal/green athletic creature runner.  
Not a normal human runner. Small alternate-species / fox-like sporty companion, teal-green athletic palette, headband, running kit and clear forward-running pose. It should read lean, fast and energetic rather than chunky. Preserve the creature identity while making later stages increasingly athletic and powerful.

## Evolution contract — next step

The approved Level 6 characters are the identity anchors. Build five visible milestone stages for each:

- **Level 2:** early / weak / inexperienced identity
- **Level 4:** first meaningful development
- **Level 6:** **this approved baseline**
- **Level 8:** expert, noticeably more equipped/powerful
- **Level 10:** Master — deliberately extravagant, rare and unmistakably final

Intermediate score numbers can use animation, energy, lighting and room-state changes without requiring a totally new character mesh/image for every number.
