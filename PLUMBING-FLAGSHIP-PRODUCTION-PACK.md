# Premium Plumbing Flagship — Production Pack

Last refreshed: 2026-09-07 by ChatGPT (OpenAI)

## Purpose

Turn `site-plumbing-flagship-v1.html` from a strong CSS/story shell into a coherent premium commercial demo without wasting Higgsfield credits or generating unrelated images.

Core principle: **one website world, one recurring technician, exact website slots first, motion only after still approval.**

## Locked creative direction

- Emergency/urgency is the opening hook; the brand must still read as premium allround plumbing/installations.
- Premium shell, authentic Dutch tradesperson.
- 90% believable commercial photography / 10% controlled cinematic polish.
- White, stone, chrome, glass/water and deep signature red.
- No slapstick flooding, panic faces, cheesy thumbs-up tradespeople or fake luxury uniforms.
- Real project/client/team claims stay real. AI imagery is atmosphere/demo storytelling, not fabricated proof.

## Master technician — PL-CHAR-001

### Role

One person who can appear across hero, arrival, inspection, repair and handover. The site should feel like one production, not five stock photos.

### Character brief

- Male trade professional, approximately 30–40.
- Dutch / Northwestern European commercial context, but avoid an over-modelled fashion look.
- Calm, competent, approachable face.
- Athletic/normal working build, not bodybuilder.
- Short practical hair, light natural facial stubble optional.
- Dark charcoal premium workwear, minimal branding, small deep-red accent.
- Clothing must look genuinely usable by a plumber/installer.
- Clean enough for a premium campaign but never pristine in a way that feels fake.
- Neutral expression baseline so the same identity can move from urgency to calm resolution.

### Master reference composition

Generate the first approved identity as a clean three-quarter portrait in a bright premium bathroom / installation environment. Hands visible if possible, natural work posture, no extreme lens distortion.

### Master prompt intent

> Premium commercial campaign portrait of an authentic experienced Dutch plumbing and installation technician in a high-end contemporary bathroom, calm confident expression, dark charcoal professional workwear with a restrained deep red accent, realistic skin and fabric, clean natural grooming, cinematic but believable daylight, white stone, chrome and glass materials, premium automotive-ad level art direction applied to a real tradesperson, no glamour-model pose, no stock-photo smile, highly consistent facial identity, room for website crop and text.

### Avoid

- Hard hat unless scene genuinely requires it.
- Suit, polo-shirt sales rep or luxury-fashion styling.
- Oversized logos.
- Orange safety vest as default identity.
- Tool belt packed with implausible tools.
- AI-perfect teeth / influencer face.
- Random age/ethnicity/facial changes between scenes.

## Slot map

### PL-HERO-001 — Bathroom emergency master still

**Purpose:** immediate comprehension + premium first impression.

Scene:
- Beautiful contemporary bathroom.
- Controlled but obvious leak/water issue near sink/pipe/fitting.
- No catastrophic flood.
- Visual tension from water/reflections, not screaming human reaction.
- Prefer no technician in the first hero candidate; keep the problem readable.
- Large quiet negative-space region for headline and CTA.

Crops required:
- Desktop: 16:10 or wider, subject/problem weighted to right side if text is left.
- Mobile: 4:5 or 3:4 with problem still readable above fold.

Approval gate:
- Problem understood in <2 seconds.
- Still looks like a premium service brand, not insurance damage stock photography.

### PL-STORY-001 — Contact / phone bridge

**Purpose:** convert visual problem into action.

Scene options:
1. Hand holding phone in same bathroom, service call screen intentionally generic/no fake number.
2. Close-up of wet fitting/background while hand reaches for phone.

Do not make the phone UI the hero; this is a 1–2 second bridge.

### PL-STORY-002 — Service vehicle arrival

**Purpose:** show responsiveness + branded professionalism.

Scene:
- Clean modern service van, realistic for a Dutch trade business.
- Master technician arriving / opening side door / taking compact tool case.
- Deep red branding accent only; avoid fake readable company registration details.
- Contemporary Dutch residential street or premium home setting.

Website crop:
- Wide 16:9 for scroll stage.
- Technician identity must still match PL-CHAR-001.

### PL-STORY-003 — Inspection / diagnosis

**Purpose:** move emotion from urgency to competence.

Scene:
- Master technician crouched/leaning naturally near affected plumbing.
- Realistic inspection light / compact diagnostic tools.
- Homeowner may be absent or soft-background only to protect identity consistency.
- Hands/tool orientation must be physically plausible.

### PL-STORY-004 — Repair in action

**Purpose:** primary craftsmanship visual.

Scene:
- Same technician actively replacing/tightening a fitting, pipe, valve or under-sink component.
- Strong hand/tool detail.
- Do not use sparks or construction theatrics that do not belong to plumbing.
- Work area organized, believable minor mess, protective cloth/case visible.

### PL-DETAIL-001 — Technical macro pack

Generate only after style lock.

Needed macros:
- Hand + chrome fitting.
- Wrench / tool engaging fitting.
- Water bead/reflection on premium chrome.
- Copper/metal/modern pipe texture.
- Tool case / ordered equipment detail.
- Red workwear accent near white stone/chrome.

Use these for section breaks, masks, micro-motion and premium texture—not proof claims.

### PL-STORY-005 — Clean-up / handover

**Purpose:** make service quality tangible.

Scene:
- Master technician wiping/cleaning the work surface or packing tools.
- Space visibly neat and dry.
- Calm daylight.
- Optional subtle homeowner presence, but technician remains visual anchor.

### PL-END-001 — Resolved end state

**Purpose:** relief + final CTA.

Preferred options:
1. Beautiful dry functioning bathroom detail with technician leaving frame.
2. Technician calm at doorway / finished environment behind him.
3. Close premium fixture/water-flow payoff with clean text space.

Do not invent a happy customer testimonial visually.

## Allround service set — PL-SVC-001

Needed so the flagship does not read as emergency-only:
- Bathroom installation/renovation.
- General plumbing maintenance.
- Pipe/fitting replacement.
- Sanitary installation.
- Heating/water-system service only where the eventual client actually offers it.

For demo imagery, generic illustrative scenes are acceptable. Once adapted to a real business, remove services the client does not actually perform.

## Proof system — PL-PROOF-001

Never generate fake project proof.

Build the premium frame/system around real client content:
- before image;
- after image;
- location/service label if client approves;
- factual short project description;
- optional date;
- truthful review only with source/permission.

The shell can be premium; the evidence must remain authentic.

## Motion gate

No video/motion generation until a still passes the still rubric.

### Still rubric — score 0–2 each

1. Identity consistency.
2. Physical realism.
3. Authentic tradesperson feel.
4. Premium brand fit.
5. Exact website composition/crop usefulness.
6. Story clarity.

Maximum 12. **Animate only 10/12+ stills.**

### Motion candidates

**PL-MOTION-001 — Hero:**
- subtle water movement/reflection;
- very slow camera drift/parallax;
- no dramatic flood growth;
- text area must remain stable.

**PL-MOTION-002 — Arrival/inspection/repair transitions:**
- short 2–4 second motion loops or transitions;
- preserve technician identity and hand/tool anatomy;
- natural speed, no music-video camera moves.

**PL-MOTION-003 — Macro texture:**
- controlled water bead / chrome light movement;
- ideal for lightweight section transition/background.

## Batch order

Do not generate the whole list at once.

### Batch A — identity lock
1. PL-CHAR-001: 3–5 controlled candidates.
2. Select exactly one master identity.
3. Run one consistency test: same person, different working pose/environment.
4. If identity consistency fails, solve this before story images.

### Batch B — critical website stills
1. PL-HERO-001.
2. PL-STORY-002 arrival.
3. PL-STORY-003 inspection.
4. PL-STORY-004 repair.
5. PL-STORY-005 handover.
6. PL-END-001.

Select winners before macros.

### Batch C — supporting texture/services
1. PL-DETAIL-001 macro pack.
2. PL-SVC-001 allround set.
3. Any prospect-specific hero test only after a real sales need appears.

### Batch D — motion
Only selected 10/12+ stills.

## Credit discipline

- Do not run broad “make it better” rerolls.
- Change one defect at a time.
- Do not animate rejected stills.
- Do not generate many different technicians for variety.
- If a scene is visually strong but unusable in the site crop, reject it for production purposes.
- Before each paid generation, record the exact slot and acceptance criterion.

## Integration order into `site-plumbing-flagship-v1.html`

1. Replace CSS hero placeholder with selected PL-HERO-001.
2. Replace generic stage technician silhouette with the locked PL-CHAR-001 scene sequence.
3. Map story beats to PL-STORY-001 through PL-STORY-005.
4. Add PL-DETAIL-001 only where it increases rhythm/readability.
5. Keep proof placeholders until real client material exists.
6. Add motion as progressive enhancement; page must still sell with motion disabled.
7. Re-run iPhone/mobile performance and comprehension check before calling the flagship production-ready.

## Production-ready definition

The flagship is ready to use in real outbound when:
- hero and story stills form one coherent visual world;
- same technician remains recognizably the same person;
- mobile first screen is clear in under 3 seconds;
- call-to-action is obvious;
- allround positioning is visible after the urgency hook;
- no fabricated proof/phone/reviews/certifications exist;
- page still works without motion;
- a prospect can immediately imagine their real company identity inside the system.
